# Example: Investment Decision

## Situation

$50K in savings earning 0.5% APR. Considering stock market but nervous after seeing parents lose money in 2008. 28 years old.

## AI Simulation

### Option A: Do Nothing (keep in savings)
- 10-year real value: ~$39K (lost $11K to inflation)
- Risk: 0% market risk, 100% inflation loss

### Option B: Lump sum into S&P 500
- 10-year range: $25K (crash scenario) to $160K (bull market)
- Typical: $100-120K
- Risk: HIGH short-term, LOW long-term (market always recovered)

### Option C: Dollar-cost average ($2K/month) ✅ RECOMMENDED
- Smooths volatility, builds habit, you'll actually do it
- Beats inflation, low regret

## Decision Brief
- **Your tendency**: Do nothing (fear-based inaction from vicarious trauma)
- **Recommended**: DCA $2K/month
- **Bias alert**: Availability heuristic — parents' 2008 loss is vivid but not representative
- **Key insight**: The biggest risk isn't a market crash. It's inflation quietly destroying cash.
